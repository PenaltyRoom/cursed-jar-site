Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information Cursed Jar ("the game") uses and why. The game is made by Fadil Ahmed ("we", "us"), in the United Kingdom.

The short version:

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or age.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. We do not run servers and receive none of your game data.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. They are never personalised, and the game never asks to track you.
  • Apple and Google handle every payment. We never see your card details.

1. What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: coins, creatures, upgrades, levels, missions, achievements, daily streak, settings and which purchases you own. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it; Settings → Reset progress erases it too.

Farm reminders: if you allow notifications, the game schedules one reminder on your device for when your Farm has been earning for 8 hours. It is created on your device; no notification server is involved, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a short ad between some runs, never in your first session and never if you buy Remove ads. You can also choose to watch an ad for a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ads are always non-personalised: they are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps or websites.
  • The game never asks Apple’s “Allow tracking” question and does not use your device’s advertising identifier for tracking.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for everyone, and Google is told to treat every ad request as child-directed under US children’s privacy law (COPPA), whatever the player’s age.
  • In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown, as Google requires; you can change them later in Settings.

To show ads, count them, limit how often you see the same one and prevent fraud, Google collects information such as your IP address (which gives a rough location), device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. Google is responsible for that information: see policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple’s App Store or Google Play and follow their terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game on your device which products your account owns, so they can be restored.

4. Game Center (iPhone)

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, your daily and best scores are sent to Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboards, shown with your Game Center name according to your Game Center settings. Apple runs Game Center; we do not receive your Game Center details. You can sign out of Game Center in your phone’s settings.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera. There is no chat and nothing you type is sent anywhere. We do not sell or share personal information.

6. Children

The game is suitable for everyone. Because we do not collect personal information, children can play without sharing any. Ads are never personalised and are limited to content suitable for everyone. Parents can turn off in-app purchases and limit ads tracking in the phone’s settings (Screen Time on iPhone, Family Link on Android). If you have any concern, contact us.

7. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. We hold none from the game itself; if you email us, we keep your email only to reply and delete it within 12 months of the matter being closed. For information Google collects for ads, use Google’s controls described in section 2. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

If you live in California or another US state with privacy laws: we do not sell or share your personal information.
